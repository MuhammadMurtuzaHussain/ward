A child makes this file in Ward and chooses to give it to you. This page checks that the messages weren't edited after they were saved, then lays out the case.

What the checks prove, and what they don't

The digest is SHA-256 over the whole report: every message, the conversation facts, the accounts and patterns, and the child's own answers (whether they're in danger now, what they want, who it's for, the details they shared). If anything in this file was changed after Ward made it, the digest won't match. Reports from before October 2026 (ward.report/v1) cover only the messages and conversation facts; the viewer says so when it opens one.

Each message's fingerprint is SHA-256 of sender, time and text, taken when the child saved the chat in Ward's locked vault. A match means that message is exactly as saved.

What neither proves: that the chat export itself was genuine. If it matters, ask the child to show you the conversation in the app. Times come from the child's chat export and are shown in this computer's time zone.

Only hurtful messages from others are in a report. The rest of the chat, the child's own messages, and anyone who stood up for them stay on the child's phone.
