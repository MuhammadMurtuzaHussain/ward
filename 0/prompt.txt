  - **What it is:** `preload.cjs` exposes `window.wardDesktop`, with `gemma.status/download/cancel/remove/generate/onProgress` and `lab.status/set`. The window keeps `contextIsolation: true`, `nodeIntegration: false` and `sandbox: true`.
